Illustration & Concept Art for Games, Books and Brands

Maybe it’s a game, a book, a brand or a short film, and right now it only lives in your notes and in your head. My job is to get it out of there. Send me whatever you have: a page of notes, a rough sketch, a long voice message. I’ll turn it into characters, places and scenes you can put in front of people.

  • Illustration: book covers, editorial pieces, posters and key art.
  • Character design: heroes, mascots and whole casts, from first sketch to final render.
  • Environment concept art: background painting and locations for games, animation and comics.
  • Storyboards: shot-by-shot boards for ads, film and animation.
  • Banners and web visuals: site headers, social graphics and campaign artwork.

I draw to your brief, so the style, mood and palette come from your project. No reference yet? We’ll find one together.
